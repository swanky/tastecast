# Limitations and honest caveats

TasteCast is a demo. These are the ways its output can mislead if read too literally.

1. **Affinity is not purchase probability.** Qloo affinity says an audience over- or under-indexes on something. It does not say anyone will buy.
2. **Characters are not real users.** A character's "taste" is a set of seed entities chosen by the operator. Every endorsement says `#ad · AI-generated character`, and the license states it is not a testimonial.
3. **Taste overlap is weak, so it has zero weight.** Shared Qloo tags between brand and character are usually 0 to 0.1. Weighted tag affinity (`filter.results.tags`) was tried in milestone 2 and disagreed with the entity signal: the tags are generic adjectives. Both are shown only as context.
4. **Itzel and Ari have nearly identical audience vectors.** For youth brands their age and gender cosines are within 0.003 of each other. The Qloo taste index is what separates them. In milestone 2 (artist and brand seeds only): Gentle Monster +3.33σ vs +1.94σ, Innisfree +1.96σ vs +0.39σ. On the live site (all four seed types, milestone 3): Gentle Monster +2.66σ vs +1.94σ, Innisfree +2.03σ vs +0.39σ.
5. **Niche tiebreak is operator data.** It only acts when the gap is under 0.03 and is labelled in the UI as *not Qloo data*. In milestone 1 it decided two of the three demos. Since milestone 2 it fires on none of them.
6. **The fit set is small, so fit has zero weight.** The explainability fit check compares the reference brand with the house brands, and Qloo returns 3 results, not 4. It stays as context.
7. **The taste index is new and lightly validated.** The everyday-brand panel (Starbucks, Nike, Uniqlo, IKEA, Patagonia, L.L.Bean), the 0.6 / 0.4 weights, the ±3σ mapping and the 0.02 SD floor were all chosen during milestone 2 on 9 briefs (3 demos plus 6 sensitivity briefs), then frozen. Milestone 3 checked them on 18 briefs and added TV and film seeds (items 16 and 17). Only the 0.08 margin and the two-measure rule were written down before any test run.
8. **The coffee demo is a close call.** For Blue Bottle Coffee, the spread-adjusted index picks Itzel and raw lift picks Ingrid. The UI shows both as viable instead of claiming a winner. Carhartt is also a close call.
9. **The campaign city does not change the score.** City-conditioned affinities sit on a different scale from the global baseline (+2 to +5σ for every seed), and Qloo sometimes returns no affinity at all for a character's seeds in a city. The city adds a no-weight in-city line instead.
10. **The Drift Check tolerance was set after seeing the data.** The first rule (strict peak bucket) flagged Itzel, whose seeds peak one bucket younger than her stated 25–29. We added an "adjacent" level (1 bucket = adjacent, ≥2 or non-positive stated-bucket affinity = drift). Ari stays at drift: she is 23, but her seeds peak at 30–34.
11. **Ingrid's female affinity is slightly negative (−0.03)** for her seed set, even though she is styled for a female-skewing audience.
12. **Places can include landmarks or generic venues.** Place results come from Qloo and are not curated. Other brands' shops and department stores are left out (item 25). For a product that is not a hospitality one, hotels, bars, members' clubs and caviar-type dining are left out too, and public places and cafés are listed first. The rules read Qloo's place kinds and a few name patterns, so a venue with unusual tags can still slip through, and when few places survive the shot list falls back to "a spot that reads <Qloo tag>" in the city. The four stored demo packages were built before this filter and show the old locations until they are rebuilt (0 Qloo calls, see README).
13. **Reference brand, not your brand.** Small brands are usually not on Qloo (for example Hario returned nothing), so the brief asks for a well-known reference brand. The ranking describes that reference brand's audience.
14. **Copy is template-first, with an optional checked AI rewrite.** Workers AI (Llama 3.3 70B) may rewrite five copy lines (the lead angle line and the four script lines) once per stored package, given only that line's cited Qloo evidence. A checker rejects any rewrite that adds a name, a brand from the evidence that the line did not already mention, or a number, or that uses first-person or testimonial wording, talent or licensing wording ("featuring", "starring", "licensed"…), wording that says the character uses the product ("daily", "routine"…), writes evidence ids, drops the disclosure or the "or similar" of a soundtrack direction, or runs past 260 characters. A rejected line keeps the template text, and if the AI call fails the whole package stays on the template. Evidence ids always come from the template line. The UI and agent run show which mode was used.
15. **Prices are illustrative.** The tier base × channel weight × duration factor is a demo formula, never charged.
16. **TV and film seeds are in the taste index (milestone 3).** Coverage rose from 10 to 12 of 12 seeds, but the Qloo-decided rate dropped from 9 to 8 of 18 briefs, and the Gentle Monster demo is now a close call (0.071).
17. **Close calls are common.** 10 of 18 real briefs are close calls. This is the honest state of the signal for three characters whose Qloo audiences overlap. It is not a UI bug.
18. **Expectations are ours, not ground truth.** On 4 of 18 briefs Qloo picked someone other than the character we expected (for example Fender → Ingrid). On Fender, Ingrid wins on audience similarity (cosine +0.82 against −0.42 and −0.20), not on taste: her index is only +0.37σ and her raw lift is slightly negative (−0.008), but it is still the highest of the three under both measures. We report these and do not tune them away.
19. **Qloo `/search` never says "unknown" for multi-word names.** It returns nearest names. TasteCast marks exact matches and warns when there are none, but the entity still has to be confirmed. The server can check that a candidate came from the search; it cannot tell whether a person or an agent picked it, and the agent run says which path was used (person, demo link, MCP agent).
20. **Images are web-size copies.** `public/assets/` ships ≤1280 px JPEGs (CC BY-NC 4.0) and the OFL fonts; the source library stays private.
21. **Fits on character pages come from the cached test briefs**, so they change when new briefs are run.
22. **The AI copy checker guards names, brands, numbers, first-person, talent and use claims, not adjectives.** A rewrite may add lowercase style words. On the demos these came from the cited Qloo tags, but the checker does not enforce that.
23. **MCP rank_roster on a custom brief spends the shared public budget** (40 calls total, per network 5 searches and 1 ranking a day). Demo briefs cost nothing.
24. **Instant brands only cover eleven names and the generic brief.** They return at once because their Qloo results are stored. Adding a campaign city, or using any other reference brand, makes live calls under the public budget. A niche tiebreak can change the verdict only when scores are within 0.03.
25. **Department stores are never shoot locations.** Places inside or named after a department store (El Palacio de Hierro, Liverpool in Mexican cities, Harrods, Selfridges, Isetan, Shinsegae and others) are moved to "left out", like other brands' shops. The list is hand-written, so an unlisted chain can still slip through.
26. **The AI copy arrives a few seconds after the result.** A fresh run shows the template copy first ("AI rewrite pending") and the checked rewrite replaces it when ready, usually within 10 seconds. If the rewrite never lands, the template copy stays and is retried after a minute.
27. **The "Without Qloo" comparison is off.** An LLM-only package for each demo brief is stored, but the panel stays hidden (`S5_PANEL = "off"`) until a blind rating exists. No rating result is shown or invented.
28. **The public custom-brief budget is small.** The project cap (300 calls) and the public allowance (40) are self-imposed, not Qloo's limit. At 241 calls used, about 59 remain, enough for roughly four custom briefs with a new reference brand (13 calls each). Raising the caps waits on confirming the real quota with Qloo. The 4 demos and 11 instant brands are not affected.
